# Interview Guide

## Introduction
In this section, we will cover the essential aspects of preparing for interviews in the software development field.

## Key Topics
1. **Technical Skills**: Focus on core programming concepts, data structures, and algorithms.
2. **Behavioral Questions**: Prepare for questions that assess your soft skills and culture fit.
3. **Mock Interviews**: Practice frequently to build confidence and improve your performance.
